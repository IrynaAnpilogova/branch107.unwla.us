We at Ukrainian National Women’s League of America(UNWLA), Branch 107, are thrilled to invite you to Ukrainian Christmas in Saratoga. We’re excited to bring together our vibrant community to celebrate Ukrainian traditions through food, crafts, music, and dance, and we are proud to be showcasing local talents and businesses. The festival will feature a unique selection of goods from Ukrainian artisans and vendors, creating a wonderful opportunity for everyone — from those looking to explore Ukrainian traditions to those simply seeking a festive holiday atmosphere.

UNWLA Branch 107 is a nonprofit recognized under IRS Code Section 501(c)(3) with Federal Identification Number: 22-2530749.

All UNWLA proceeds from this festival will be used to support these UNWLA humanitarian initiatives: Emergency Response to the War in Ukraine: Medical Aide and Outreach Mission - Healing Pediatric Burns

Thanks to all our friends who support our humanitarian efforts and who generously donated wine for our fundraiser. Thank you to our sponsors: Summerwood Winery in Paso Robles, Uphill Wineyard in Amador County and House Family Vineyards in Saratoga.

Pysanka Workshop

Join us for a special pysanka class led by Sofia Ogorodnyk, a skilled Ukrainian artist. Sofia will guide us in creating unique Christmas-themed designs, inspired by traditional holiday stars. All materials and designs will be provided by the artist—come ready to enjoy this meaningful craft rooted in Ukrainian tradition!

11:00 a.m. - 1:00 p.m.

2:00 p.m. - 4:00 p.m.

Book workshop
Ukrainian Block Printing Workshop

Explore the art of 'vybiyka' at this amazing workshop! Learn the traditional techniques of printing patterns using dyes applied to hand-carved wooden blocks and stamping them on fabric with oil art paints. Join us to create beautiful ethnic designs! This class for adults and teens only.

2:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
